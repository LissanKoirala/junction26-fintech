Junction 2026 concept · not an official S-Pankki product · all data is synthetic, and Aino is a fictional customer. Every shop, brand, product and review is invented by the app; none is a real retailer. The shop's text is written live by Gemini (Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, streaming structured output); the product photos come from a separate image model. The coach answers with Gemini and function calling, and never does the arithmetic: an engine computes every figure and a guard checks it. The eight machine-learning models are our own, trained on the challenge data. Product footage is recorded from the live prototype, edited for length; the build stopwatch shows the true elapsed time and any sped-up part is labelled. Money movement in the prototype is simulated: nothing ships, no card is charged, and the wallet button is Stripe in test mode, never pressed. Narration is a synthetic voice (ElevenLabs); the music and effects are original generated audio.
