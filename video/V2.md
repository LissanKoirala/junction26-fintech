# Vara videos, version 2: the contract

Two videos for the Junction 2026 submission, rebuilt because the first version (fourteen narrated slides) was
judged "boring and slow, no wow factor". The owner approved the script below on 15 Nov 2026 and asked for:
Crave mode as the star, a real demonstration of a shop being built on the fly, no awkward gaps between
sentences or scenes, background music and effects that build momentum, and screens that are not dense.

| Video | File | Hard limit | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demo | `vara-demo.mp4` | under 2:00 | about 1:50 |
| Pitch | `vara-pitch.mp4` | under 5:00 | 4:15 to 4:40 (shorter is fine; never pad) |

Both are 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, h264 + stereo AAC, one continuous edit each (no slide numbers, no holds).

## Rules (all three builders and the verifier)

**Pace.** A scene lasts as long as its narration plus at most 0.3 s; the next line starts within 0.15 to
0.3 s of the last. The only silent beats are the two marked `[BEAT]`. Something on screen changes at least
every 2 s. Cuts land on the music's beat where that costs nothing.

**Density.** One idea per screen. Outside the phone, at most about ten words of on-screen text at a time, in
large type. No tables, no paragraphs, no list of more than three items visible at once; numbers appear one
at a time, each big and alone. If a scene needs more, split it into more screens, never shrink the type.

**Honesty (unchanged from version 1; the first video failed a check on these once).**
- Every figure comes from `DECK.md` / `SOURCES.md`. No new facts, no rounding beyond the script's.
- No real shop or brand name may be visible in any frame (the Crave intro's "Shopping Lab" card names real
  shops: never show it; scan every recording for brand words as `scripts/record-app.mjs` already does).
- The deck draws no S-Pankki or Apple logo and never writes or says "Apple Pay". A wallet button inside a
  recording of the live app is allowed.
- A small persistent label on every frame: "Vara · Junction 2026 concept · not an official S-Pankki product".
- On app footage: "Live app recording · edited for length · synthetic data" (and "×N speed" on any stretch
  that is sped up). Where Aino is named: "Aino is a fictional customer".
- During the pay and twist scenes, on screen: "Prototype: money movement is simulated".
- The 72% scene carries: "A replay of two years of data, all else unchanged. Not a prediction."
- The build stopwatch shows the true elapsed time of the real build, even where the footage is sped up,
  and marks both the moment the shop opens and the moment it is complete.
- Nothing may claim more than the prototype does (shops are invented, not real retailers; Säästäjä and
  open banking are mocks; Stripe is test mode; six of eight models are experimental).

**Secrets and outside services.** Keys live only in `~/proj/.env` on the VM: never print, log, copy or commit
one. Only narration text and generic music / sound prompts go to ElevenLabs. No personal details anywhere.

**Machines.** The local machine is tiny: never run node, npm, npx, tsc, ffmpeg or ffprobe here; small
`python3` text scripts only; under 600 MB of disk is free, so pull only what the repo needs (footage, audio,
a few downscaled stills). Heavy work runs on the Azure VM, from `/home/lissan/fin-junction`:
`bin/vm '<cmd>'` (rsync --delete the local repo to `~/proj`, then run), `bin/vmx '<cmd>'` (run without
syncing), `bin/pull '<path relative to VM home>' <local dir>/`. Because of the `--delete`, never leave an
output under `~/proj` on the VM: write to your own folder (`~/vidrec2`, `~/vidnar2`, `~/vidout/v2`) and pull
what the repo needs into the local tree. Three builders share the VM and the local tree at once: touch only
the files you own.

**Do not** edit `app/`, `shopping-lab/`, `ios/` or anything outside `video/`; do not deploy, publish, commit
or push (the orchestrator does those after verification). Version 1's files stay as they are.

```json
  "sfx": { "whoosh": { "file": "v2/audio/sfx-whoosh.m4a", "seconds": 0 } } }
```
Narration: the same narrator as version 1 (voice id in `scripts/narrate.py`), read faster and with more
energy (about 165 to 180 words a minute, still natural), one file per scene so the sentences run together;
leading and trailing silence trimmed to 60 ms or less; no pause inside a file longer than 0.35 s; sentence
times measured on the final file. Music: an original instrumental (no vocals, no existing song, no
artist's name in the prompt) that builds: modern, warm, confident, a clear pulse around 110 to 124 bpm;
at least 5 minutes of it for the pitch (it is faded at the end) with a recognisable lift for the build and
the twist; the demo may use the same track. Effects, all generated or synthesised: at least two whooshes,
a riser of about 3 s, an impact / hit, a soft tick for numbers, a pop for on-screen chips. The app's own
pay chime, basket pop and coins already exist in `video/public/audio/` (rendered from the app's code): keep
using those for the phone's sounds.

## The mix (EDIT)
Voice always intelligible: music sits about 18 to 22 dB under the voice and rises between lines and on
montages. Before the pay chime the music stops dead (riser into silence), the chime plays alone with
nothing spoken within 1.5 s either side, and the music returns on the word "twist" / "nothing ships" with an
impact. The final file is normalised to about −16 LUFS, true peak under −1 dB.
