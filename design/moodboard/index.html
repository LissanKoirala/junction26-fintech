Design moodboard · for approval

Vara, the money trainer that feels like it was always part of S-mobiili.

The palette, type scale, spacing and shapes below are taken from S Group's own design system, which ships in the bank's public CSS. What needs your decision is how much personality Vara gets inside that system: three directions for the main screen, and three options for Vara's mark.

Junction 2026 concept · not an official S-Pankki product  Figures in the mock-ups are illustrative, except the balance and rent, which are the demo customer's real values.

Three words

Every screen should pass these three tests.

Calm

One number per screen that matters. Mint background, white cards, lots of air. No traffic-light red and green for ordinary spending.

Native

Looks like S-mobiili: same greens, pill buttons, thin outlined icons, Finnish money format (1 743,50 €), and the real five-tab bar around it.

Encouraging

A trainer, not an auditor. Plain words, concrete euros, one small next step. Small wins get noticed.

3 · Vara's mark

A small avatar that appears in the header, on the home card and next to chat answers.

Option 1 · Sprout

Growth from small things. Friendliest, closest to S-Pankki's "start with a cup of coffee" saving tone.

Option 2 · Rising V

The letter V drawn as a tick that turns into an upward line. Most "trainer"-like; reads well at tiny sizes.

Option 3 · Speech spark

A chat bubble with a spark: says "AI you talk to" most directly, and echoes the app's existing chat icon.
